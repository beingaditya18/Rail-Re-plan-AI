# Station Platform Allocation, Berthing & Turnaround Rules

## 1. Platform Berthing Constraints
- Length Clearance: Standard 24-coach passenger trains require platforms of minimum 600 metres length. Vande Bharat (8/16 coach) requires 200m/400m platforms.
- Freight Berthing: Freight trains shall NEVER be berthed on passenger island platforms unless specifically designated for parcels or emergency holding on dedicated goods lines/loops.
- Buffer Time: A minimum clear interval of 3 to 5 minutes between the departure of a train and the arrival of the next train on the same platform face is mandatory for route locking release and passenger dispersal.

## 2. Rake Turnaround & Watering
- Primary Terminal Maintenance: Minimum 4 to 6 hours for pit line examination.
- Secondary / Intermediate Turnaround: Minimum 30 to 45 minutes for quick turnaround trains without primary maintenance.
- En-route carriage watering: Mandatory 10 to 15 minutes halt at designated watering stations (e.g. Jhansi, Mathura, Agra Cantt).

## 3. Platform Change Restrictions
- Platform changes announced within 15 minutes of train arrival cause passenger stampede hazard on foot overbridges (FOB).
- Any platform change must be accompanied by audible public address announcements in Hindi and English, physical display board updates, and SMS alert dispatch.
