# Indian Railways Operating Manual - Track Maintenance & Line Block Protocol

## 1. Line Block Definition
- A line block or power block is an authorized closure of a specific track section or overhead electrical traction (OHE) for engineering maintenance, machine tamping, rail replacement, or emergency derailment clearing.
- No revenue train may enter the blocked section without a pilot order or written authority to proceed (T/369-3b or pilot memo).

## 2. Temporary Single Line Working (TSLW) on Double Line
- When one line of a double-line section is blocked:
  - Movement in the wrong direction on the clear line is governed by Temporary Single Line Working (G&SR 9.15).
  - Train speeds on the single line are capped at 25 km/h for the first train (pilot train) and 40 km/h for subsequent trains.
  - Crossovers between UP and DN lines shall not exceed 30 km/h (standard 1 in 12 turnouts) or 15 km/h (1 in 8.5 turnouts).
- Station controllers must sequence UP and DN trains alternately or in batches of 2-3 to minimize crossing deadlocks.
- Opposing movements must be regulated at stations with adequate loop lines (e.g. Mathura, Agra Cantt, Gwalior, Jhansi).
