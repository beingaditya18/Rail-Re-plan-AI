# Railway Board Operating Directives - Train Priority and Precedence Hierarchy

## 1. Statutory Train Hierarchy Order
In the event of line disruption, regulation, or single-line working, Section Controllers and Chief Controllers must accord precedence in strictly descending order:
1. **Priority 1 (Weight 100)**: Vande Bharat Express & Gatimaan Express (High-speed semi-bullet services).
2. **Priority 2 (Weight 95)**: Rajdhani, Shatabdi, Duronto Express (Prestige long-distance express).
3. **Priority 3 (Weight 70)**: Superfast Express / Garib Rath / Humsafar / Tejas Express.
4. **Priority 4 (Weight 50)**: Standard Mail / Express / Jan Shatabdi services.
5. **Priority 5 (Weight 30)**: Passenger / MEMU / DEMU commuter trains (regulate in loops to clear high-speed corridors).
6. **Priority 6 (Weight 10)**: Freight trains (Container / BCN / BOXN / POL / Coal). Freight can be stabled in common loops indefinitely to recover passenger punctuality.

## 2. Punctuality Catch-Up Guidelines
- A premier train (Vande Bharat / Rajdhani) running late by up to 45 minutes should be given through green aspects to utilize make-up margin.
- If a lower priority train is running on-time and an overtaking high-priority train is heavily delayed (> 90 min), controller must evaluate whether detaining the on-time train destroys overall network throughput.
