# Hours of Employment and Period of Rest (HOER) - Running Crew Regulations

## 1. Running Staff Duty Hours
- Running staff (Loco Pilots, Assistant Loco Pilots, Train Managers / Guards) fall under the 'Continuous' or 'Intensive' classification under the Railway Act, 1989.
- Maximum continuous duty on footplate:
  - Normal duty: 9 to 10 hours from signing ON to signing OFF.
  - Absolute emergency ceiling: 12 hours. Any extension beyond 10 hours requires concurrence from Senior Divisional Operating Manager (Sr. DOM).
  - Exceeding 12 hours is a serious safety violation under Commissioner of Railway Safety (CRS) guidelines.

## 2. Relief and Spare Crew Callout
- If a train is delayed en route and projected to exceed 10 hours before reaching crew-changing station, relief crew must be ordered at intermediate crew points.
- Designated crew changing and spare lobby stations on the NDLS-BPL corridor:
  - New Delhi (NDLS) - Lobby A & B
  - Mathura Jn (MTJ) - Emergency sub-lobby
  - Agra Cantt (AGC) - Primary crew change hub
  - Gwalior (GWL) - Running room & standby pool
  - Jhansi Jn (JHS) - Divisional HQ crew depot
  - Bina Jn (BINA) - Freight & express relief pool
  - Bhopal Jn (BPL) - Terminal lobby
- Minimum callout warning required for standby spare crew is 90 minutes.

## 3. Mandatory Rest Period
- Mandatory rest at outstation running rooms: Minimum 8 hours continuous rest.
- Mandatory rest at headquarters (home station): Minimum 16 hours continuous rest (or 12 hours under emergency roster).
