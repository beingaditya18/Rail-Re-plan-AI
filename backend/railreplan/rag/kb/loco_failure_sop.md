# Indian Railways Operating Manual - Locomotive Failure & Section Clearing SOP

## 1. Incident Classification
- Loco failure in block section: Total loss of tractive effort or brake pipe pressure drop stopping the train mid-section.
- Crew Action: Loco Pilot must immediately switch ON the flasher light, sound 4 short whistles, and secure the train with hand brakes. Assistant Loco Pilot must protect the train with detonators at 600m and 1200m as per G&SR 6.03.

## 2. Relief Engine Dispatch
- Station Master of adjacent station must requisition an assisting locomotive (light engine or freight power from nearby yard) within 15 minutes of failure notification.
- Clearing the section to the nearest station siding or loop has absolute priority over through movements to prevent cascade stranding of following trains.
- In double line sections, assisting engine can be worked into the blocked section under Authority T/A.602.
