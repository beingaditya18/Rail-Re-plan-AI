# Northern & North Central Railway - Fog Operating Procedures (FOP)

## 1. Foggy Weather Classification & Visibility Test Object (VTO)
- When visibility is impaired due to fog, mist, dust storm or heavy rain such that the station Visibility Test Object (VTO) located 180 metres from the platform cannot be seen by the Station Master on duty, foggy weather working is declared.
- Fog Signal Posts (FSP) located 270 metres ahead of the first stop signal must be manned or FOG-PASS devices engaged.

## 2. Speed Caps During Fog
- During dense fog (visibility < 150m):
  - Maximum permissible speed of trains fitted with modified Fog PASS (GPS based driver assistance device) is capped at 60 km/h, reduced to 30 km/h on yellow aspect or non-interlocked points.
  - Trains without working Fog PASS device must restrict speed to 30 km/h, prepared to stop at any obstruction.
  - Rajdhani and Vande Bharat trains are strictly limited to 60-75 km/h depending on track-circuit integrity.

## 3. Section Capacity Degradation
- Section transit time increases by 40% to 120% during dense fog.
- Station controllers must introduce staggering of departures to prevent bunching at outer home signals.
- Freight operations should be throttled or held in loops to keep mainline clear for premier passenger services.
