# CRIS / IRCTC Passenger Communication & Disruption Notification Directive

## 1. Statutory Notification Threshold
- Any train running behind schedule by 30 minutes or more must trigger automated SMS and App Push notifications to all reserved passengers.
- For replanned station arrivals, any change exceeding 5 minutes or ANY change in assigned platform number must immediately trigger bilingual alerts.

## 2. Bilingual Mandate
- In North Central and Northern Railway zones, all emergency alerts must be issued in both Hindi (Devanagari script) and English.
- The tone must be informative, objective, stating revised expected time of arrival/departure, platform number, and general cause (fog, maintenance block, operational reasons) without causing panic.

## 3. Communication Channels
- Multi-channel delivery: SMS (primary for low-bandwidth / feature phones), IRCTC RailConnect Push Notification, Station Public Address (PA) auto-chime, and Electronic Display Boards (EDB) at concourse and platforms.
