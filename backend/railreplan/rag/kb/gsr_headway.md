# Indian Railways General & Subsidiary Rules (G&SR) - Headway and Block Signalling

## 1. Automatic Block Signalling Headway
- Minimum permissible separation between consecutive trains travelling in the same direction on standard double-line automatic block signalling territory is governed by 3-aspect or 4-aspect signal spacing.
- The default operational planning headway is 5 minutes for Mail/Express trains, and 7 minutes behind heavy freight or non-airbrake rakes.
- Under degraded signalling or caution orders, minimum headway expands to 10-15 minutes depending on visibility and train speed.

## 2. Station Overlap and Signal Clearing
- Block Overlap (adequate distance beyond Starter or Home signal) of 180 metres (2-aspect) or 120 metres (multi-aspect colour light) must be kept clear before taking 'OFF' reception signals.
- No second train shall be admitted to a station section until the previous train has cleared the fouling mark and berth track circuit.

## 3. Precedence at Junctions
- When two trains approach a converging junction simultaneously, the higher priority train as per Railway Board operating directives shall be admitted first.
- If the lower priority train is already on the approach circuit within braking distance, it shall not be brought to an abrupt stop unless safety demands.
